## Project summary

The Student Portal System provides students with a single platform to access academic information and manage important student-related activities. It aims to make common academic tasks easier for students and reduce the effort required to find information in different places.

## Short answers

**1. What made you choose this problem?**

Students often need to access academic information and manage different college-related activities. When information is spread across different platforms, it can take extra time and effort to find what they need. We chose this problem to make student-related tasks simpler and more convenient through a single portal.

**2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?**

The system is designed for college students who need convenient access to academic information and student-related services. During a normal week, students could log in to check available information, manage relevant academic activities, and use the portal's features instead of switching between multiple platforms. The goal is to make routine college tasks easier to organize and access.

**3. If you had another month, what would you build next?**

We would improve the system based on student feedback and add more useful features. We would focus on reliable data storage, better user access controls, and improved performance. We would also test the portal with students and refine the interface to make it easier to use.
